# Every link gets a share image

## What

10 of 31 sampled routes unfurled with no image. Eight set their own `openGraph`
and lost the root image. `/lists` went through the Worker, which skipped the tags
when it had no art. All of them now carry one, and `/kodi` gives the add-on a page
to share, because a zip URL can never have a preview.

## Mistakes

- Sent the supporter message with the zip URL. That is a file, so it cannot unfurl.
- Assumed the root layout's image covered every page. `twitter` hid the gap: its
  image kept working on seven of these routes while `og:image` was gone.

## What worked

- Reading the tags off production across every route before fixing, which found
  the two year pages and `/lists` that a grep of `app/` page files would have missed.

## Rules

- A route that declares `openGraph` declares `images` too. `tests/og-images.test.ts`
  fails otherwise.
- Share a page, not a file.
